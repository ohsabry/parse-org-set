Sample Extractor for ORG 24

Extract the audio samples inside ORG 24 .Set and .Instrument files, as WAV files organized by instrument.

Runs entirely in your browser: your file stays on your computer, so nothing is uploaded.

Made by @ohsabry · Source on GitHub · MIT

Not affiliated with Sofeh Sunrise or ORG 24. The format was worked out from a handful of files, so check your sounds.
